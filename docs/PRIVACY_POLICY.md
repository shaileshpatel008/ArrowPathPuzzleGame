## 1. Information stored on your device
Your game progress (levels, stars, statistics, streaks, settings, hint balance)
is stored **only on your device**. We do not run servers that receive it. If
Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back this data up to
your Google account under Google's terms.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use
information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate
location), device and app usage information to deliver, personalise and
measure ads. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states we ask for your
consent through Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any
time in **Settings › Privacy & ad choices**. Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. In-app purchases
Purchases are processed by **Google Play**. We never receive your payment
details. Google Play shares with us only the information needed to deliver
your purchase (product, order and purchase token).

## 4. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## 5. Your choices
- Change ad consent: Settings › Privacy & ad choices
- Reset or limit your advertising ID: your device settings
- Delete local game data: Settings › Reset progress, or uninstall the app
