Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Path Puzzle (the "App"), published on Google Play by Shailesh Patel ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

To run the game, the App saves the following on your device: levels completed, stars, statistics, daily-challenge streaks, settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, accessibility options) and your hint balance and purchase status. This data is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back this data up to your Google account under Google's terms. You can delete it at any time using Settings › Reset progress in the App or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, limit, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads watched) and diagnostic data.

Ads are not shown to Arrow Path Plus members. Ad content is restricted to a family-friendly rating.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & ad choices. If consent status cannot be determined, only non-personalised ads are requested.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads on most Android devices).

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Hint packs, the Theme Pack and Arrow Path Plus (monthly, yearly or lifetime) are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We receive only the information needed to deliver and restore your purchase (product, order status and a purchase token). We do not receive your card or bank details.

5. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store on our own servers any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or files. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage or precise location.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you reset progress or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google for ads and payments is retained under Google's policies. You can request deletion of Google-held data through your Google Account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent and to object to personalised advertising. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through the in-app privacy options, your device settings or Google. You can also contact us and we will help.

9. Security

Data exchanged with Google's advertising and payment services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Game data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

10. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than yours, using the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
